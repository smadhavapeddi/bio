Senior AI Solutions Architect II, DigitalOcean

I work at the intersection of infrastructure and applied AI. As a Solutions Architect, my role is to sit alongside customers and internal teams while they design, prototype, and ship AI and machine learning systems — translating open-weight models, GPU infrastructure, and platform services into working products that hold up in production. That means architecting end-to-end solutions, building reference demos like the ones below, and helping teams reason clearly about the trade-offs between cost, latency, and scale, so the right call gets made early rather than fixed later.
